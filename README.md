## 🔬 Key Projects & Research

### 🧠 Brain Tumor Segmentation & Automated Reporting
> **Tech**: CNN, U-Net, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, FastAPI, AWS/Azure. 
Built a deep learning segmentation pipeline for clinical brain MRI, achieving **93% test accuracy** while extracting volumetric and morphological biomarkers (core, edema, active margins). Paired findings with fine-tuned transformers to automate clinical report drafting, cutting physician documentation time by **35%**.

### ⚡ Clinical EEG Analysis & Seizure Detection Workflows
> **Tech**: EEG, CHB-MIT Dataset, PyTorch, Biostatistical Modeling  
Engineered reproducible predictive pipelines on clinical EEG recordings with automated artefact suppression and feature engineering[cite: 1]. Developed benchmarking protocols assessing algorithm sensitivity, specificity, and generalization across diverse patient cohorts.

### 🔋 Non-Invasive Glucose Prediction from Multimodal Signals
> **Tech**: ECG, PPG, Signal Processing, Feature Engineering, XGBoost, Scikit-learn  
Designed clinical study protocols across 1,000+ patient sessions. Extracted 39 handcrafted time-, frequency-, and morphological-domain features to mitigate sensor baseline drift, achieving **89.9% classification accuracy** via nested cross-validation and building an interactive data-ingestion GUI.

### 🩺 Institutional DICOM Imaging & 3D Reconstruction Pipeline
> **Tech**: DICOM, NIfTI, Multi-Planar Reconstruction (MPR), Python, Linux  
Architected an institutional diagnostic imaging pipeline handling **1,500+ slices per study**, featuring memory-optimized multi-planar reconstruction (axial, sagittal, coronal) and 3D volumetric rendering for clinical review.

### 🔪 AI-Based Surgical Skill Evaluation
> **Tech**: Vision Transformers (ViT), PyTorch, Applied Mathematics, Biostatistics 
Formulated a Vision Transformer architecture to benchmark and classify surgical proficiency (novice, intermediate, expert)[cite: 1] by modeling spatial-temporal tool–tissue interaction dynamics.

### 🎗️ Computer-Aided Breast Cancer Screening from Full-Field Mammograms
> **Tech**: Scikit-learn, OpenCV, Scikit-image, Python.  
Developed a computer-aided screening pipeline detecting micro-calcifications and structural margin anomalies, achieving a **90% F1-score** across normal, benign, and malignant classifications while minimizing false negatives.
